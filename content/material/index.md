# Selected Materials

Material for revising the SQR and QAM lectures, and the reading behind them.

| Page | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [SQR Study Materials](sqr/index.md) | A study note (SN) and revision questions (RQ) for each bachelor lecture, L01–L10 |
| [QAM Study Materials](qam/index.md) | The same for each master lecture, A01–A10 |
| [Reading List](reading-list.md) | Textbooks and key papers, one annotation per entry |

A study note follows its lecture in order and links to the handbook pages that hold the detail. The revision questions test understanding rather than recall; answers are folded under each question.

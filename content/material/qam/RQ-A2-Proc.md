# RQ A02: Process Improvement

These questions test [SN A02: Process Improvement](SN-A2-Proc.md). Explain in your own words and give brief examples; for calculations, show each step.

---

## Question 1: The Credibility Gap

What is Humphrey's "credibility gap"? Name two things managers typically say they value and the practice that contradicts each. Why does Humphrey object to relying on heroics?

---

## Question 2: Quick Fix or Process Fix

For each of the three business situations in SN §1 (recurring customer-visible bugs, being beaten to market, missed milestones on fixed-price work), state the quick fix and the process question SPI would ask instead.

---

## Question 3: Columbia

How did organizational culture contribute to the loss of Columbia? Give one example of the same mechanism in a software team.

---

## Question 4: The One-Eighth Rule

1. Name the three filters of Pfeffer's one-eighth rule and show how they give 12.5%.
2. For each filter, describe one countermeasure.

---

## Question 5: Single and Double Loop

Explain single-loop and double-loop learning. A release ships late; give one single-loop and one double-loop response.

---

## Question 6: PDCA

Apply PDCA to reducing the number of bugs found by customers. State a measurable target in Plan and what each later step does.

---

## Question 7: Kaizen

What are the four principles of Kaizen as presented in the lecture? Name two software practices that embody them and say which principle each shows.

---

## Question 8: Worse Before Better

A team delivers late; management requires overtime; three months later two developers quit and delivery is later still.

1. Explain what happened using work harder, work smarter and the capability trap.
2. What is the self-confirming attribution error, and how does it keep management on the same path?
3. What should management have done instead?

---

## Question 9: Defect Prevention Process

1. Name the four components of IBM's DPP and what each does.
2. What are the four cause categories used in a causal-analysis meeting?
3. What results and cost did IBM report?

---

## Question 10: Theory of Constraints

Changes in a team wait three days for code review, while coding a change takes one. Apply the five focusing steps to this situation, with one concrete action per step.

---

## Question 11: Six Sigma

1. What does "six sigma" mean in defects per million opportunities?
2. Name the five DMAIC phases and the question each answers.
3. Why does Six Sigma suit a CI build process better than the design of a new feature?

---

## Question 12: DPP versus Retrospectives

Compare DPP and Agile retrospectives by trigger, question asked, input and who acts on the result.

---

## Question 13: CMMI Structure

1. What is a process area, and how do specific and generic goals differ?
2. How do generic goals make a process survive deadlines and staff changes?
3. What is the difference between the staged and the continuous representation?

---

## Question 14: CMMI Levels

1. Name the five maturity levels and describe each in one sentence.
2. What is the key difference between Level 1 and Level 2?
3. What changes from Level 3 to Level 4, and which process areas does Level 4 add?

---

## Question 15: CMMI Process Areas

List the seven Level 2 process areas with their abbreviations. How many process areas does each level add, and what is the total? Which two belong to Level 5?

---

## Question 16: CMMI and Agile

At which maturity levels do Agile practices fit CMMI well, partly or not at all, and why? Which Level 2 area is usually missing in Agile teams?

---

## Question 17: Judging CMMI Results

A vendor slide claims that CMMI v2.0 gives 17% better estimation accuracy, 70% less rework and 97% on-time delivery. Where do these figures come from, and how much weight would you give them?

---

## Question 18: Two Levels of Change

Describe Andrews and Stalick's operational and cultural levels of change. Give an example of an SPI initiative that fails because only the operational level changed.

---

## Question 19: Success Factors and Barriers

1. Which success factor do studies of Six Sigma, Lean Six Sigma and ITIL rank first, and why?
2. List the three adoption barriers from the Bangalore CMMI study with their percentages, and propose a mitigation for each.
